# LoudLoft for Windows and Linux

The installers for the LoudLoft desktop app. Get the one for your computer from
**https://loudloft.com/download**, which also lists each file's SHA-256 checksum. Every release
here has its files and what changed.

LoudLoft is a place to talk, call and share your screen with your lofts and your friends:
https://loudloft.com

Questions or problems: support@loudloft.com
